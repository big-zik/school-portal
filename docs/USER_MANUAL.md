# 📖 School Portal User Manuals

Comprehensive user guide for all stakeholders at St. Augustine International Academy.

---

## 🎓 1. Student User Manual

### Sign In
1. Navigate to the portal homepage: `/`
2. Click **Sign In to Portal** or use the **Student 1-Click Demo** button (`student.daniel@school.com` / `Password@123`).
3. You will arrive at your personal **Student Dashboard**.

### Weekly Timetable
- Click **Class Timetable** on the sidebar.
- View class periods, assigned lecture rooms, and instructor names for each day from Monday to Friday.
- Click **Print Timetable** for an offline hardcopy.

### Assignments & Submissions
1. Click **Assignments** in the navigation bar.
2. Select any active task from the list.
3. Review instructions, rubric, and download attached materials.
4. Drag and drop your completed file (PDF or DOCX) into the upload dropzone and click **Upload & Submit Assignment**.
5. Once your teacher grades the assignment, view your score, percentage, and teacher commentary directly in your dashboard.

### Examination Results & Report Cards
- Click **Grades & Results**.
- View your continuous assessment (40%) and examination (60%) breakdown across all registered subjects.
- Check your class rank and terminal GPA.
- Click **Download PDF Report Card** to download an official cryptographic PDF transcript.

## 👨‍👧 2. Parent / Guardian Manual

### Linking & Monitoring Children
1. Sign in with your parent account (`parent.johnson@school.com` / `Password@123`).
2. Your dashboard displays summary cards for each of your linked children (e.g., Daniel Johnson).
3. View real-time attendance percentage, academic average, and fee balances at a glance.

### Paying School Fees Online
1. Click **Fee Invoices & Pay** on the sidebar.
2. Review the itemized breakdown of tuition, lab fees, and e-learning resources.
3. Click **Pay Fees Online** to open the secure Paystack checkout modal.
4. Complete the transaction via card, transfer, or USSD.
5. Once settled, instantly download your official bursary receipt (`REC-2025-00089`).

### Booking Teacher Conferences
- Click **Teacher Meetings** to book one-on-one appointments with your child’s subject teachers or form tutor during designated conference hours.

## 👨‍🏫 3. Teacher User Manual

### Taking Daily Period Attendance
1. Sign in with teacher credentials (`teacher.smith@school.com` / `Password@123`).
2. On your **Teacher Dashboard**, click **Take Attendance** next to any scheduled period.
3. Mark students as **Present**, **Late**, **Excused**, or **Absent**.
4. Click **Mark All Present** for fast batch roll-call.
5. Click **Save Attendance**. The system automatically triggers absence alerts to parents.

### Creating & Grading Assignments
1. Click **Assignments & Tasks** -> **New Assignment**.
2. Set title, instructions, class cohort, due date, and total marks.
3. Upload assignment rubric or reference files.
4. When students submit, open the **Pending Grading** queue, enter marks, write feedback, and click **Publish Marks**.

---

## 🏫 4. Administrator Manual

### Student Admissions & Enrolment
1. Sign in with admin credentials (`admin@school.com` / `Password@123`).
2. Navigate to **Students** -> **Admit New Student**.
3. Fill out student biodata, assign an admission number, cohort, and link guardian information.

### Academic Broad Sheets & Result Approvals
1. Navigate to **Examinations & Reports**.
2. Inspect class broad sheets, grade distributions, and failure alerts.
3. Click **Approve Results** to release report cards to student and parent dashboards.
