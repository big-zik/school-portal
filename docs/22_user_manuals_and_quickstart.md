## 2. Student User Manual

### 2.1 First-Time Login & Password Reset
1. Open your browser and navigate to `portal.school.edu.ng/login`.
2. Enter your student email address (or admission matriculation number) and initial temporary password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. Upon first login, navigate to **Settings** and update your password to a strong, private password containing at least 8 characters.

### 2.2 Accessing Your Timetable & Homework
1. In the sidebar, click **Timetable** to view today's periods, classroom locations, and teachers.
2. Click **Assignments** to view pending homework tasks.
3. Click on any assignment to view instructions. Type your response or upload a PDF/image file, then click **Submit Assignment**.

### 2.3 Taking a Timed CBT Examination
1. Click **CBT Quizzes** in the left menu.
2. Select an active quiz and click **Enter Test Room**.
3. Read the exam instructions carefully. Once ready, click **Start Test**.
4. The live countdown timer will begin. Select your answers (A, B, C, or D). Use the bottom question palette to jump between questions.
5. Click **Submit Test** when finished, or wait for the timer to auto-submit when it hits 00:00.
6. Your score and performance breakdown will appear immediately.

### 2.4 Checking Your Results & Report Cards
1. Click **Results** in the sidebar.
2. Select the current academic session and term.
3. If your school fees are fully paid, your subject scores, grades (A1 to F9), teacher comments, and GPA will be displayed. Click **Download PDF Report Card** to save or print.
4. *Note:* If you see a **Fee-Based Result Lock**, notify your parent or guardian to settle your outstanding tuition balance.

## 3. Parent / Guardian User Manual

### 3.1 Creating an Account & Linking Your Child
1. Visit `portal.school.edu.ng/register` to create a parent account.
2. Log in and navigate to the **My Children** section.
3. Click **Link Child / Ward**, enter your child's student admission number and date of birth, and click **Verify & Link**.
4. Your child's profile, attendance status, and fee statements will immediately link to your portal.

### 3.2 Checking Daily Attendance & Academic Progress
1. Open your parent dashboard at `/parent`.
2. Toggle between your children using the student tabs at the top.
3. View the attendance card to see whether your child was marked Present, Late, or Absent today.
4. Click **Results** to view published CA continuous assessment scores and termly report cards.

### 3.3 Paying School Fees Online (Paystack)
1. In the parent sidebar, click **School Fees**.
2. Select the unpaid invoice and review the itemized breakdown.
3. Click **Pay with Paystack**.
4. Choose your payment method (Debit Card, Bank Transfer, USSD, or QR).
5. Once your transaction completes, the system marks your invoice as **PAID** immediately and generates an official downloadable payment receipt.

### 3.4 Uploading Bank Transfer Receipts (Offline Payment)
1. If you made a bank transfer or cash deposit directly at a bank branch, open **School Fees**.
2. Click **Upload Bank Receipt**.
3. Enter the issuing bank name, teller/transaction reference number, amount paid, and upload a clear photo or PDF of your receipt.
4. Click **Submit for Approval**. The school bursar will verify the payment against the school bank statement and approve it within 24 hours.

## 4. Teacher User Manual

### 4.1 Taking Morning Attendance
1. Log into your `/teacher` dashboard.
2. Click **Attendance** and select your assigned class and today's date.
3. All enrolled students are marked **Present** by default.
4. Click to toggle any student who is **Absent** or **Late**.
5. Click **Save Attendance**. Parents of absent students are notified automatically.

### 4.2 Recording Continuous Assessment (CA) Scores
1. Click **Grades / CA Entry** in the sidebar.
2. Select your class and subject (e.g. JSS 2A - Basic Science).
3. In the score sheet, enter **CA 1** (max 20 marks), **CA 2** (max 20 marks), and **Exam** (max 60 marks).
4. The system automatically computes the Total (0-100) and assigns the correct WAEC letter grade (A1 to F9).
5. Click **Save Grades**.

### 4.3 Creating Assignments & CBT Tests
1. Click **Assignments** -> **New Assignment** to publish homework with due dates and attachments.
2. Click **CBT Exam Desk** to author multiple-choice quizzes with custom time limits and point weights.

## 5. Administrator & Bursar User Manual

### 5.1 Approving Bank Payment Receipts (Bursar)
1. Log into `/admin/fees/approvals`.
2. Review the pending receipt queue showing parent names, student IDs, amounts, and uploaded bank slips.
3. Cross-reference with the school's bank statement.
4. Click **Approve** to clear the invoice and generate an official receipt, or click **Reject** with a feedback reason.

### 5.2 Running Staff Monthly Payroll
1. Navigate to `/admin/payroll`.
2. Click **Run Monthly Payroll**, select the current month and year.
3. The system computes Basic Pay, Allowances, 8% statutory Pension deduction, and PAYE tax withholding.
4. Review the breakdown and click **Approve & Execute Payroll**.
5. Digital payslips are generated for all academic and administrative staff.
